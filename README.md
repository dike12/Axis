# Axis Finance 🪐

> **Built for the people. Engineered for real life.**

Axis is a personal wealth manager built for the reality of money—not the way accountants wish it worked.

Most budgeting apps are designed for people who already have a cushion. They shame you for buying coffee while ignoring the real question: *"If I lose my job tomorrow, how long can I survive?"*

Axis is different. It is built for those growing from zero to financial independence. It focuses on **Survival Metrics**, **Real-World Cash Flow**, and the actual psychology of money.

## 🌍 Why Axis Exists

**1. Real Life is Messy**
Getting paid on the 25th shouldn't break your monthly budget. Axis uses smart **"Income Shifting"** logic to move late paychecks to the month where you actually spend them. No more fake "rich" months followed by "poor" months.

**2. Survival > Tracking**
Knowing you spent $50 on dining out is useless if you don't know your **Runway**. Axis treats "Months of Freedom" as the most important number on your dashboard.

**3. Owned by You**
Your financial data shouldn't be sold to credit card companies. Axis is self-hosted, local-first, and completely free. You own the code, you own the data.

## 🚀 Key Features

### The "Survival" Dashboard
- **Runway Metric:** Instantly calculates how many months you can live without income.
- **FI Progress:** Visualizes your journey to the "4% Rule" (Financial Independence).
- **Debt Load:** Monitors your Debt-to-Income ratio with clear health alerts (Green/Red zones).

### The "Real World" Ledger
- **Rollover Rules:** Automatically detects income received late in the month (e.g., after the 20th) and shifts it to the next month's budget.
- **Planned vs. Reality:** A budget planner that clearly shows the "Delta"—the exact amount you have remaining or overspent.
- **Dynamic Categories:** Create categories on the fly, because life expenses change constantly.
